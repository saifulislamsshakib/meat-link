# 🥩 MeatLink

## Livestock & Meat Supply Chain Management Platform

MeatLink is a full-stack MERN-based livestock and meat supply chain management platform that connects Farmers, Slaughterhouses, Drivers, and Super Shops through a complete digital workflow.

The platform manages the process from livestock listing and procurement to livestock delivery, meat processing, packaging, publishing, and Super Shop ordering.

## 📌 Project Overview

MeatLink is designed to digitally manage the livestock and meat supply chain.

The system provides different functionalities based on the user's role.

### Main User Roles

- 👨‍🌾 Farmer
- 🥩 Slaughterhouse
- 🚚 Driver
- 🛒 Super Shop

Each role has its own dashboard, permissions, and workflow.

```text
Farmer
   ↓

   ↓
Create Procurement Request
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
Slaughterhouse Creates Meat Product
   ↓
Meat Processing
   ↓
Packaging
   ↓
Publish Product
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
Place Order

   ↓
```
